🌟 Vision Statement
Fixora exists to bridge the gap between service seekers and service providers by creating a trusted, accessible, and AI‑powered mobile marketplace. Our mission is to make finding reliable help as simple as tapping a button, while empowering providers to showcase their skills professionally.
🎯 Why Fixora Exists
For Service Seekers: People often struggle to find trustworthy providers quickly when urgent issues arise (e.g., plumbing leaks, electrical faults, or home repairs). Don't know fair pricing, long waiting time, no temporary guidance.
Fixora ensures seekers can:
Discover nearby providers instantly through location search.
Use AI camera analysis to identify problems and receive temporary DIY recommendations while waiting for help.
Book appointments, chat, and leave reviews with ease.
For Service Providers: Skilled professionals often lack the tools to present themselves effectively online. Difficult to attract customers, no easy quotation system, poor customer management.
Fixora helps providers:
Create polished profiles using AI “Text‑to‑Profile” (voice or text input).
Manage jobs, quotations, bills, and receipts in one place.
Build credibility through ratings, reviews, and repeat customers.
💡 Core Purpose
Fixora exists to connect communities with trusted services while lowering barriers for providers to enter the digital marketplace. By combining AI assistance, location‑based search, and a freemium model, Fixora makes professional help accessible, fast, and reliable.
🌍 Target Audience
Seekers: Homeowners, tenants, and individuals needing urgent or routine services.
Providers: Professionals in home repairs, electricians, plumbers, cleaners, gardeners, IT support, automotive services, painters, movers, and other skilled trades.
🚀 Long‑Term Vision
Fixora will grow from a local service marketplace into a scalable ecosystem where:
AI enhances both seekers’ and providers’ experiences.
Payments, subscriptions, and advanced features support sustainable growth.
Communities rely on Fixora as their go‑to platform for trusted services.
